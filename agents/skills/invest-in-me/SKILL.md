# invest-in-me

This is the skill of creating documents for research grant applications.

## process

1. Prepare a list of questions that are as detailed as possible, depending on the given items to be included and the information that is required.
2. Ask the user the questions one by one and collect the answers.
3. Based on the user's answers to the question, look for areas that could be explored further and create new questions.
4. After collecting all the necessary information, create a document that includes all the required items and information for the research grant application.
5. Review the document for completeness and accuracy, ensuring that it meets the requirements of the research grant application.
6. If necessary, go back to step 1 and ask additional questions to fill in any gaps in the information.

## notes

- Never delete any pre-filled questions or content.
- Responding in the language the user is using in the chat.
